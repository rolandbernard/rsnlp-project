* Collaboratively decide on a word, without saying it. Goal is to think of the same word. -> More dialogue based that most other games.
* Escape a maze. -> Requires spacial reasoning.
* Reorder blocks. -> Requires spacial/numerical reasoning. Not very dialogue based.
* Letter Boxed? -> Not really dialogue based.
* Some kind of task that must be performed, but the two players have only partial information. -> More free form dialogue based.

e.g. Letter Boxed
> You are given the following four sets of letters: {I, E, U}, {L, N, M}, {F, K, T}, {A, H, C}. You have to give me an ordered list of common english words, such that: i) all letters are in one of the sets, ii) no two consecutive letters are in the same set, iii) the first letter of one word, is the same as the last letter of the preceding word, and iv) every letter of every set appears at least once in the list of words. Your goal is to use as few words as possible. Write your output as "WORDS: " followed by the list of words separated by spaces. Do not write anything else.
